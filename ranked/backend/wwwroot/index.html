How to play ranked

  1. InstallDownload the game above and unzip it. Start it with play.cmd.
  2. Sign inOpen Ranked in the main menu and sign in with Google or Discord, then pick a nickname.
  3. Find a matchPick your faction and colour in the Ranked panel, then press Find match. Keep the game open while searching.
  4. FightWhen an opponent is found you join the game server automatically, and the game starts by itself about 10 seconds after both players have joined. The result and your new rating are recorded when the game ends.
